What has been ingested

These are historical prepared datasets, not all SEC companies, all filings or an intraday feed. Numeric rows are not unique comparable financial facts. Insider transaction rows include different transaction codes, grants and other activity, not only purchases or sales.

Settlement-failure context

Fails-to-deliver are outstanding balances on each settlement date. They are not daily new fails and cannot be added across dates to measure current exposure. They do not prove short selling or abuse. The lagged previous-day price field is not an execution-price feed.
