SEC order 34-106668, dated October 14, 2026, ordered a suspension from October 15, 2026 at 04:00 ET through October 28, 2026 at 23:59 ET. This is one source-reviewed historical case, not a live or complete halt feed.

The order identifies TJGC common stock as listed on Nasdaq Capital Market as of October 14, 2026. That is not its initial listing date or a verified current security mapping. The SEC cited potential manipulation, not an adjudicated finding.

Both source endpoints have minute precision. The offline matcher leaves both boundary minutes unknown and returns only positive interior matches for this exact CIK. Other issuers and times outside the window remain unknown, never cleared for trading.

Why the same ticker is not a continuous price history

Before the October suspension, TJGC had a separate 1-for-3 reverse split of ordinary shares effective May 26, 2026: three old shares became one new share, with post-split CUSIP G2588N116. The May 6-K says the ticker remained TJGC. The later 20-F reports 10,100,023 issued shares after rounding, versus the nominal 10,100,000 announced count.

The 20-F already restates its share and per-share financial data for that consolidation. Do not apply the ratio twice or treat it as a vendor-price adjustment factor. May 26 fell between the separately reported May 15 halt and June 3 scheduled resumption; no May 26 trade or fill is established.

A separate August 6-K announces marketplace reflection of Class A/Class B share redesignations on August 12, 2026, with Class A retaining TJGC. That is not another reverse split. Same ticker or CIK does not verify the same share class across dates.

Research check: review security identity and adjustment basis before comparing prices across these dates. The offline date-window guard flags overlapping event dates for review only. No match means unknown, not corporate-action-free. No vendor prices, adjustment factors, returns or complete corporate-action coverage are established.
