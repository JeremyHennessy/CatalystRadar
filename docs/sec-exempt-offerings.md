# Q3 2026 Form D baseline

Offline bounded ingestion of the official prepared dataset from https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-d-data-sets. No network calls or trade integration. Run `python3 backend/sec_formd_data.py --root PRIVATE_SOURCE_DIRECTORY --out NEW_PRIVATE_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` with original 2026q3_d.zip and browser-receipt.json. Use a trusted private parent outside this checkout. Output directory0700, SQLite0600. Do not publish raw source or database; related parties, addresses and signatures remain private. The no-overwrite rule is not immutable storage.

The importer preserves every field of all six tables as source text, including Indefinite values, amendments, previous-accession references, first-sale dates, offering/sold/remaining amounts and business-combination fields. It does not sum financing, reconcile amendments, infer acquisitions or label SEC approval. Previous-accession references can point outside this batch and are not asserted to have been ingested. Reporting issuer CIKs include private issuers and investment funds; counts are not a current listed-company universe or deduplicated combination with the financial/insider baseline.

Exact schemas, bounded ZIP inventory, source hash/size, unique submission/offering identities, accession joins, one primary issuer per submission and filing dates are validated. Inconsistent amendment flags are preserved in a gap table. Missing records fail closed. FULL synchronous commits and reopened counts/quick_check precede completion output; incomplete outputs must not be published. Names/addresses are never included in public summaries. Browser status and exact download clock remain null; local verification times prove retained bytes, not first publication or historical availability.

Actual source: 15,952 notices, 14,968 distinct normalized issuer CIKs, 10,291 D and 5,661 D/A submissions, filed July1–September30,2026. All six source tables persisted: 15,952 submission, 16,160 issuer, 15,952 offering, 50,014 related-person, 6,739 recipient and 16,150 signature rows. 7,201 total offering amounts are Indefinite. No amendment-form conflicts were observed. These records are historical notices, not completed rounds, recommendations or paper executions. Original paper ledger and held research reports are unchanged.
