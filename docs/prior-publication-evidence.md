# Explicit prior-posting evidence

An accepted SEC filing is an observation, not proof that its contents are new. The offline `prior_publication_evidence.extract` utility locates one narrow primary8-K text pattern: presentation materials were posted on the issuer website before the presentation commenced. It preserves the containing paragraph's original byte offsets/hash and document hash, leaving every timestamp, issuer-assertion status and independent-publication verification unknown. Surrounding text may quote or deny the pattern; the parser never upgrades a text match into a positive issuer assertion.

This is an unconfirmed text cue for manual context and novelty review, not a determination that all disclosure is old or an authenticated website posting. It cannot establish who could access the website, the exact first-public time, whether later material changed, or global economic-event deduplication. An absent match does not mean the information was new. Multiple matches, missing/amended/ambiguous primary forms, malformed document inventories and unsupported wording remain unknown; comments/scripts/styles cannot supply the cue.

The existing private financial-admission rehearsal includes this observation field but does not map it to a qualified catalyst or change trading gates. Caller validation of filing identity and retained-byte hash remains mandatory. Inputs are bounded to25MB, no network is used, all public tests are synthetic, and no source-derived results or financial values are published by this change. Held research reports and the main ledger are unchanged.
