# Q3 2026 crowdfunding and Regulation A baseline

These are two distinct official prepared SEC datasets, not an offering-success tracker. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/crowdfunding-offerings-data-sets and https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/regulation-data-sets. Download original ZIPs through normal public links. No account, private contact, paid source or source call is used by this offline importer.

Run `python3 backend/sec_small_offerings.py --root PRIVATE_SOURCE_DIRECTORY --out NEW_PRIVATE_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY`. The source directory contains both original ZIPs and browser-receipts.json. Use a trusted private parent outside the checkout. Output directory0700, SQLite0600; raw addresses, signatures, related persons and provider identities must not be published. No overwrite is permitted, but this is not immutable or independently timestamped storage.

Both original archives are checked for exact allowlisted URL/path/receipt fields, SHA256/bytecount, bounded inventory and known table headers. Every field in 22 TSV tables is preserved verbatim. Unique submission identities, filing dates, submission-type vocabulary and child accession references are verified. Empty co-issuer CIKs remain empty and are counted, never invented or added to an issuer universe. Only accession joins are claimed; no blanket table-specific or historic cross-quarter identity reconciliation. FULL synchronous commits, reopened row counts and quick_check precede completed summary. Failed output retains INCOMPLETE.

Crowdfunding contains 559 submissions across374 submission-issuer CIKs: C177, C/A162, C-U136, C-AR55, C-TR14, C-W13, C/A-W1, C-TR-W1. Do not equate statements, updates, amendments, annual reports, terminations or withdrawals with unique completed offerings. Source amendment flags and narrative context remain verbatim; no automatic override from form type.

Regulation A contains184 submissions across119 submission-issuer CIKs: 1-A44,1-A/A65,1-A POS48,1-K14,1-K/A3,1-Z10. Tables are conditional on form family, not missing rows by default:157 A-family,17 K-family,10 Z-family. Offering, annual and exit reports remain distinct. A filing alone does not establish qualification, endorsement, completed proceeds or financial suitability.

As-filed offering amounts, price, financial and period fields are not summed or converted to comparable facts. All issuer counts are separate from existing coverage and each other. Only allowlisted aggregate summary is published, not names/addresses/signatures/financial values. Exact browser HTTP status and download clocks are unavailable and remain null; local verification time is not first-public evidence. No trade activation, orders, backdated decisions, report regeneration or ledger updates.
